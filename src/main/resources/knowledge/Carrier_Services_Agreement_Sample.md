# Carrier Services Agreement

This Carrier Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between **DAP10** ("Customer") and **Carrier** ("Service Provider").

## 1. Pricing

The Service Provider will provide pickup and delivery services ("Services") subject to the terms of this Agreement. Pricing incentives ("Incentives") applicable to the Services are described in Addendum B.

These Incentives are available only to the locations and account numbers identified in Addendum A. Customer account numbers and those of affiliates that are more than fifty percent (50%) owned by the Customer may be added or removed only through mutual written agreement between the parties and will become effective after seven (7) business days' notice.

The Customer may not resell or transfer these Incentives to any third party without prior written consent from the Service Provider. Failure to comply may result in termination of this Agreement.

The Customer acknowledges that the Incentives and minimum rates described in Addendum B are based on the Service Provider's standard published rates and may change if those published rates are updated. Eligible shipments will receive the applicable Incentives during the Agreement term. Incentives are applied weekly unless otherwise specified and apply only to eligible prepaid outbound shipments unless otherwise noted.

The Service Provider reserves the right to periodically review Customer compliance with this Agreement.

## 2. Payment Terms

The Customer agrees to pay all invoices in full within the payment period specified by the Service Provider.

For accounts listed in Addendum A, Incentives are contingent upon payment through approved electronic payment methods, including ACH transfer or credit/debit card.

## 3. Services

All Services are provided in accordance with the Service Provider's current Rate Guide and Terms and Conditions of Service, which are incorporated into this Agreement by reference. These documents may be updated from time to time and, together with this Agreement, constitute the complete understanding between the parties regarding the Services.

## 4. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of this Agreement, including pricing, Incentives, commercial terms, and any discussions regarding interpretation, modification, renewal, or negotiation of this Agreement ("Confidential Information"), unless disclosure is required by law.

Neither party shall publicly disclose or distribute Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent of the other party.

## 5. Offer Expiration and Prior Agreements

This offer is valid until **[Offer Expiration Date]**.

The Customer may accept this Agreement by submitting a signed copy before the expiration date.

This Agreement supersedes any previous agreements between the parties relating to the Services described herein.

## 6. Term

The Incentives become effective on the Monday following execution of this Agreement or the Effective Date, whichever occurs later.

The Agreement remains in effect for **52 weeks**.

After the initial term, the Service Provider may extend the Agreement on a month-to-month basis.

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days' prior written notice.
