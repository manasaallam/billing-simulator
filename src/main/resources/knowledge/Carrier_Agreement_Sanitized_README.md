# Carrier Agreement Knowledge Base Entry (Sanitized)

> Purpose: Contract metadata extracted from a carrier agreement. Company names and other identifying details have been removed while preserving pricing, discount, incentive, billing, and operational terms.

## Service Overview

- Services are provided according to the applicable Rate and Service Guide and Terms and Conditions of Service.
- Service offerings may be modified by the carrier over time.
- Only approved accounts listed in the agreement are eligible for incentives.
- Account additions typically require advance written notice.
- Resale or transfer of incentives to third parties is prohibited without written approval.
- Customers may be entitled to advance notice if material service-guide changes significantly impact costs, liabilities, or contractual rights.

## Contract Administration

### Entire Agreement

- Agreement and addenda supersede prior agreements.
- Modifications require written agreement between the parties.

### Documentation Requirements

- Shipments must be supported by required shipping documentation.

## Payment Terms

- Customers must pay invoices according to the payment terms defined in the Rate and Service Guide.
- Negotiated payment terms may override standard payment terms.
- Failure to make timely payments may result in suspension of services or credit terms.

## Credit Management

- Credit limits may be assigned to customer accounts.
- Credit limits may be periodically reviewed and adjusted.
- Services may be suspended if account balances exceed approved credit limits.

## Discounts and Incentive Programs

### Incentive Types

Incentives may be provided in the form of:

- Discounts
- Discounted rates
- Waivers
- Promotional pricing programs
- Other approved incentive mechanisms

### Incentive Rules

- Incentives apply only to eligible accounts and services.
- Shipping discounts apply to base transportation rates.
- Incentives may be adjusted based on customer shipping activity.
- Incentive eligibility may depend on shipment volume and shipment characteristics.

## Customer Rates

- Customer pricing is based on the carrier's published Rate and Service Guide.
- Discounted customer rates may differ from published rates.
- Contract pricing may be reviewed periodically based on actual shipping patterns.

## Accessorial Fees and Surcharges

Customers remain responsible for applicable accessorial charges unless specifically waived in the agreement.

Examples include:

- Fuel surcharges
- Delivery area surcharges
- Residential surcharges
- Additional handling fees
- Duties and taxes
- Other accessorial service charges

## Shipping Activity Commitments

- Incentives may be based on committed shipping activity.
- Actual shipment activity may be reviewed periodically.
- Incentives may be adjusted if actual activity differs from committed activity.

## Shipment Characteristics

- Pricing and incentive eligibility may depend on shipment characteristics.
- Carriers may review weight, dimensions, destination profiles, and service mix when evaluating incentives.

## Confidentiality

- Customer rates, discounts, incentives, and related commercial terms are confidential.
- Public disclosure generally requires legal obligation or written consent.

## Liability Limits

Common logistics agreement provisions may include:

- Liability limits based on international transportation conventions.
- Declared value options.
- Per-shipment liability limits.
- Annual aggregate liability caps.
- Exclusions for willful misconduct or gross negligence.

## Cargo Withholding Rights

- Carrier may withhold cargo when undisputed invoices remain unpaid.
- Cargo may be released after outstanding balances are settled.

## Agreement Duration

- Agreement term defined by start and end dates.
- Either party may terminate through written notice.

## Governing Law

- Agreement disputes are governed by the jurisdiction specified in the contract.

## References to Carrier Rate Guide

The agreement repeatedly references:

- Rate and Service Guide
- Terms and Conditions of Service
- Published Rates
- Customer Rates
- Incentive Programs
- Accessorial Charges
- Shipping Eligibility Rules
