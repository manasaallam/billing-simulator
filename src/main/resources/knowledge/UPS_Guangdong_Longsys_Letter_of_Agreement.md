## 13. Liability Limits

The Warsaw Convention and the Montreal Convention limit UPS's liability for lost or damaged cargo unless the Customer declares a higher value and pays the applicable additional charges.

If the Montreal Convention applies, UPS's liability is limited to:

- **26 Special Drawing Rights (SDR) per kilogram**

unless the Customer declares a higher value and pays the required fees.

In all other cases, UPS's liability is limited to:

- **USD 100 per shipment**

unless the Customer declares a higher value and pays the required fees.

If the Customer, or any person from whom it derives its right to claim, has caused or contributed to any loss or damage to a shipment or package, UPS's liability may be reduced or extinguished according to the law applicable to contributory negligence.

UPS's total liability under this Agreement for each shipment shall not exceed the actual loss suffered by the Customer and its affiliates.

For each contract year, the aggregate liability of UPS to the Customer and its affiliates shall not exceed the actual losses suffered by the Customer and its affiliates.

In no event shall such aggregate liability exceed:

**USD 500,000 per contract year**

Notwithstanding these limitations, none of the limitations or exclusions in this section apply to liability arising from UPS's willful misconduct or gross negligence.

## 14. Cargo Withholding Rights

UPS agrees that its exercise of the right to withhold delivery of cargo shall be subject to the conditions mutually agreed upon in this clause.

If the Customer fails to timely pay any invoices, excluding amounts genuinely disputed in good faith, UPS shall have the right to remind the Customer to make payment.

If the Customer still fails to make payment within three (3) calendar days thereafter, UPS shall have the right to:

- Withhold cargo and documents.
- Notify the Customer in writing immediately or within twenty-four (24) hours after such withholding.

Where the Customer completes payment of all outstanding amounts after withholding occurs, UPS shall promptly release the cargo within twenty-four (24) hours.

Where UPS invoices have been timely paid or any payment default has been remedied according to this clause, UPS agrees not to exercise any right to withhold cargo or documents unless required by government authorities, customs authorities, competent regulatory bodies, or applicable laws and regulations.
