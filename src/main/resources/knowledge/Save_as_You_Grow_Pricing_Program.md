# Save as You Grow Pricing Program

## Overview

The **Save as You Grow** pricing program allows businesses to earn larger shipping discounts as their shipping volume increases.

Pricing automatically adjusts as the business scales from **10 shipments per week** to **20, 30, or more shipments per week**, ensuring customers receive the best available discounts on their most frequently used shipping services.

---

## Key Benefits

- Up to **52% off Ground Shipping** rates on all shipments.
- Up to **75% off Air Shipping** services.

## Minimum Shipping Charges

### Ground Shipping

For each package shipped using Ground Shipping, the customer will pay the greater of:

1. The discounted shipping charge based on the applicable incentive level; or
2. The published rate for a **1 lb Zone 2 package**.

### Three-Day Shipping

For each package shipped using Three-Day Shipping, the customer will pay the greater of:

1. The discounted shipping charge based on the applicable incentive level; or
2. The published rate for a **1 lb Zone 302 package**, less the applicable incentive below.

#### Additional Incentive

- **25% off** the Three-Day Shipping minimum charge.

### International Standard Shipping

For shipments using International Standard Shipping between eligible countries, the customer will pay the greater of:

1. The discounted shipping charge based on the applicable incentive level; or
2. The published **1 lb rate** for the applicable shipping zone.

## Terms & Conditions

- This pricing program outlines the incentives available for eligible shipping services.
- Discounts are based on the customer's average weekly shipping volume.
- Incentives apply only to base transportation charges unless otherwise stated.
- Additional services, accessorial fees, surcharges, taxes, duties, and other non-transportation charges are not discounted unless specifically listed in this document.
- Discounts are calculated based on the billing week of each shipment.
- Pricing tiers automatically adjust as shipping volume changes.
- Discounts apply only to the designated shipping account and are non-transferable.
- Resale or redistribution of discounted shipping services is prohibited unless expressly authorized.
- The shipping provider reserves the right to modify, suspend, discontinue, or terminate this pricing program, in whole or in part, at any time without prior notice.
- Continued use of shipping services constitutes acceptance of the current pricing program and applicable service terms.
