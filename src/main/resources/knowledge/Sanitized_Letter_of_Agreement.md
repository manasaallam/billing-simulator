## Agreement Introduction

This Agreement contains the basic terms under which **[Service Provider Legal Name]** (the **"Service Provider"**) and **GD EXPRESS SDN BHD** (the **"Customer"**), being authorized to represent the entities listed in Addendum A, agree to the following:

---

## 1. Service Overview

Service Provider shall provide services to Customer as set out in the applicable service guide, rate schedule, or service terms in effect at the time of shipment, as amended by Service Provider from time to time.

Customer agrees that Service Provider may alter its service offerings at its discretion.

All goods carried are subject to the following prevailing at the time of shipment:

1. The applicable terms and conditions of carriage.
2. The applicable rate and service guide, including any limits of liability set out therein.

Only letters, documents, paks, and packages shipped under the account number(s) listed in Addendum A are eligible to participate in the designated incentives.

Only those eligible shipments will be used to determine whether Customer has met the minimum requirements of this Agreement.

If a shipment document does not indicate one of the specified account numbers in Addendum A, Service Provider may be unable to apply the incentive in billing Customer.

Account numbers may be added or deleted only by mutual written agreement between the parties. Additions to Addendum A require ten (10) business days advance notice to become effective.

Customer is prohibited from reselling or offering incentives to any other party without the prior written consent of Service Provider. Failure to comply may result in immediate cancellation of this Agreement and revocation of any discounts or incentives provided to Customer.

## 2. Entire Agreement

This Agreement and all Addenda supersede any existing agreements made between the parties relating to the subject matter of this Agreement.

It may only be modified by written agreement between the parties.

Both parties confirm that before signing this Agreement they have carefully read its contents, understood their rights and obligations, and agree to this Agreement, its Addenda, and the applicable terms and conditions referenced herein.

---

## 3. Documentation

Service Provider will only accept shipments if accompanied by adequate documentation as specified in the applicable terms and conditions of carriage and applicable service guide.

---

## 4. Payment Terms

Customer agrees to pay for all shipments in full within the time period required by Service Provider.

The negotiated payment terms for the applicable account numbers are identified in Addendum A. These payment terms supersede any previous payment terms for these account numbers.

Payment terms contained within this Agreement will be valid for the term of this Agreement, after which time the accounts will revert to the payment terms contained in the then-current service guide, tariff, or terms and conditions of service.

The following payment terms apply:

1. Bill shipping charges to shipper/third party for export freight prepaid shipments: **30 days**.
2. Bill shipping charges and duties/taxes/levies to shipper/third party for export free domicile shipments: **30 days**.
3. Bill shipping charges and duties/taxes/levies to receiver for import freight collect, duties, and taxes shipments: **30 days**.

---

## 5. Non-Transferability

Customer agrees that the discounts and terms of this Agreement are only applicable to the account numbers listed in Addendum A.

The discounts and terms may not be assigned, transferred, or used for resale to any other party without prior written agreement between the parties.

Customer understands that breach of this clause entitles Service Provider to immediately cancel this Agreement.

## 6. Confidentiality

Customer agrees to keep confidential and not publish or publicly display the terms, discounts, and/or customer rates set out in this Agreement, including their existence, conditions, and amounts, unless disclosure is required by law or agreed in writing by Service Provider.

## 7. Discount Program

The specific details of the discounts covered by this Agreement are listed in the attached Addendum B (**"Discounts"**).

The Discounts will apply to the applicable base shipping rates as stated in the applicable rate and service guide in effect at the date of the shipment.

Service Provider will apply the appropriate Discounts and Rates only to the services set out in Addendum B.

Customer will pay applicable surcharges and accessorial charges as published in the applicable rate and service guide, unless otherwise indicated in Addenda B and C or as otherwise agreed in writing by Service Provider.

Deficit weight may not apply to accounts listed in Addendum A if the account is exempt.

---

## 8. Automation

Customer agrees to supply Service Provider with a hard copy summary manifest at the time packages are tendered for shipment and provide timely upload of electronic package level detail (**"PLD"**) in a form acceptable to Service Provider.

PLD includes, but is not limited to:

- Consignee full name.
- Complete delivery address.
- Package weight.
- Zone.

Timely upload is defined as the electronic transmission of PLD to Service Provider at the time packages are tendered.

Customer agrees to provide approved shipping labels on all packages tendered to Service Provider.

Customer further agrees that all shipping locations will use a shipping solution that is approved and authorized by Service Provider.

## 9. Duration

This Agreement will remain in effect from **09 January 2019** until **14 November 2047**, and will automatically continue thereafter for an indefinite period unless and until terminated by either party in accordance with this Agreement.

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, including during the first year, without cause by giving thirty (30) calendar days written notice.

## 11. Shipment Characteristics

The incentives granted in Addendum B are based on the shipping characteristics described by Customer.

Service Provider reserves the right to renegotiate any discounts based on Customer actual shipment characteristics, which may be analyzed periodically.

Notification shall include contacting a Service Provider representative and providing shipment characteristics, including dimensions, weight, and destination.

Service Provider reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to modify discounts or operating agreements for shipments exceeding **2,000 kgs**.
